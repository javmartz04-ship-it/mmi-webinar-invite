Nobody Taught Me Either.

I owned a landscape construction company for 22 years. I built it with my hands, ran it well, and made real money. I built my own home and raised my family in it. By every measure, I was doing well.

What I never learned were the rules about investing, or protecting what I built. My dad was never a pilot. If I wanted to fly, I’d have to go learn it myself. Money works exactly the same way.

Then I spent two years at the qualification table of investing seminars, watching good people realize the money just wasn’t there. It wasn’t a money problem. It was a rules problem. So I started teaching the rules myself.
